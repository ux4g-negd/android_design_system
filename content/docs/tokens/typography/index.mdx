# Typography Overview

UX4G Typography is calibrated to provide optimal visual legibility across multilingual citizen applications in Android Jetpack Compose.

All text styles are organized into 5 primary categories accessible via `Ux4gTheme.typography`:

---

## Typography Categories

- **[Header Tokens](/tokens/typography/header)**: `Ux4gTheme.typography.h*` (`hXXS` to `hXXL`) for view titles and prominent section headings.
- **[Display Tokens](/tokens/typography/display)**: `Ux4gTheme.typography.d*` (`dXS` to `dL`) for hero landing banners and celebratory numbers.
- **[Body Tokens](/tokens/typography/body)**: `Ux4gTheme.typography.b*` (`bXS` to `bL`) for standard reading paragraphs and descriptions.
- **[Label Tokens](/tokens/typography/label)**: `Ux4gTheme.typography.l*` (`lS` to `lXL`) for buttons, form labels, tags, and chips.
- **[Title Tokens](/tokens/typography/title)**: `Ux4gTheme.typography.t*` (`tS` to `tL`) for card headlines and list titles.
- **[How to use](/tokens/typography/how-to-use)**: Detailed usage guide and Compose examples.
