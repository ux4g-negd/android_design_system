# Service Discovery & Booking

Enables citizens to find relevant government departments, search schemes, and book physical appointment slots.

---

<h2><MIcon name="search" /> Key Modules</h2>

1. **Global Service Search**: High-contrast search input with instant matching.
2. **Category Service List**: Grouped scheme directory (Agriculture, Health, Education, Revenue).
3. **Appointment Time Slot Booking**: Grid of morning/evening time slots with instant confirmation.
