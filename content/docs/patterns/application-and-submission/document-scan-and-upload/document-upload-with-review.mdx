# Document Upload with Review

An automated document inspection and quality review pattern presented when citizen attachments are flagged by pre-submission AI checks or statutory rules for verification issues such as blurriness, missing self-attestation, or edge truncation.

Rather than rejecting an entire application downstream or halting the citizen abruptly, this pattern highlights the specific attachment requiring attention (*e.g., Residence proof flagged with warning styling*) alongside successfully approved attachments. Explicit **View** and **Re-upload** actions enable the citizen to inspect what was flagged and supply a clearer scan before final submission, while progress indicators reflect partial completion (*3 of 4 uploaded, 1 flagged / 75%*).
