# Document Upload Success

A completed intake confirmation pattern presented when all mandatory citizen attachments and voluntary proofs have been successfully received and validated during the documentation phase of a government petition or entitlement application.

The pattern validates citizen effort by reaching 100% on the quota indicator (*All required documents uploaded — 4 of 4*), displaying an affirming success banner, presenting verified document receipt cards with itemized metadata (*Aadhaar Card, Proof of Income, Residence proof, and Caste certificate*), and enabling seamless advancement via **Proceed to review**.
