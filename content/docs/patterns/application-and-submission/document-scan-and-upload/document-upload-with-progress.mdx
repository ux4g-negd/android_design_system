# Document Upload with Progress

An active upload feedback pattern indicating real-time transmission of citizen attachments during the documentation stage of government schemes and statutory applications.

When a citizen attaches heavy files—such as multi-page salary slips, tax returns, or identity credentials—this pattern provides responsive feedback via an in-card transmission progress indicator (*e.g., Uploading income_proof.pdf... 65%*), an hourglass activity indicator, and an immediate **Cancel** option to abort a stall. Simultaneously, overall quota progress dynamically recalculates (*Required documents — 2 of 4 uploaded / 50%*), offering reassurance while subsequent requirements remain accessible.
