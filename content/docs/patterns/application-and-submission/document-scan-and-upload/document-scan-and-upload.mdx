# Document Scan and Upload

A structured multi-document intake pattern designed for government schemes, statutory certificate requests, and entitlement applications requiring verifiable citizen attachments.

The pattern streamlines complex document requirements by providing an overarching upload progress bar (*e.g. 1 of 4 uploaded*), real-time upload affirmation cards for completed attachments (*Aadhaar Card with file metadata, View, and Re-upload actions*), and dual acquisition pathways for pending requirements—direct camera/file scan upload alongside seamless one-tap integration with **DigiLocker** for instant statutory verification.
