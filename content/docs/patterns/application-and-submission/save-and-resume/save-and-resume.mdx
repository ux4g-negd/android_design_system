# Save and Resume

A resilient workflow pattern that safeguards applicant progress during multi-step government service requests, license applications, or certificate submissions.

When citizens return to an incomplete or previously saved application, the system surfaces a prominent status banner detailing the active draft, current step milestone (`Step 3 of 5 Document Upload`), and last saved timestamp. Citizens can choose to either **Resume** exactly where they left off or **Discard** the draft to begin a fresh application.
