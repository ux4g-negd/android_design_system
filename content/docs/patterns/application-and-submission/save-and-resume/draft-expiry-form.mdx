# Draft Expiry Form

A time-sensitive form persistence pattern that informs applicants of draft retention policies and countdown expiration thresholds.

To avoid data loss from server retention cleanups, multi-step government service forms display an urgency banner indicating the remaining valid lifetime of a draft (*"Your draft expires in 5 days"*) alongside the exact calendar cutoff date (*"16 Apr"*). Citizens can continue form completion immediately or manually trigger **Save draft** to refresh their session without advancing stages.
