# Eligibility Final Question Step

The concluding questionnaire step in an eligibility verification workflow, capturing terminal qualification criteria before triggering the automated eligibility determination logic.

At this final stage, the progress bar reflects 100% completion (*Question 5 of 5*), signaling to the citizen that their assessment is almost complete. The interface displays categorical status options covering past application history (e.g. expired certificates, existing active validity, or first-time applicant status). The primary CTA transforms into a definitive submission trigger (*"Check Eligibility"* or *"Continue"*), transitioning the citizen seamlessly to their eligibility determination outcome.
