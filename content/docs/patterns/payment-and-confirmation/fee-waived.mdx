# Fee Waived

An essential affirmative action and social entitlement confirmation pattern presented to citizens who are legally exempted from application, registration, or processing fees (e.g., Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), Persons with Disabilities (PwD), or economically weaker sections under government concession rules).

The pattern prevents applicant confusion regarding payment status by explicitly communicating that a 100% waiver has been applied to their application, detailing every statutory line item (base fee, processing charges, GST) alongside the waiver adjustment, confirming a total payable balance of **Rs 0.00**, and offering a single-tap progression without requiring payment gateway checkout.
