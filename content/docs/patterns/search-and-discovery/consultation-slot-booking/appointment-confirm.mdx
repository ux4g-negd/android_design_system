# Appointment Confirm

A post-booking success acknowledgement pattern confirming finalized consultation reservations, delivering reference audit numbers, and offering post-booking next actions.

The pattern reassures the citizen upon completion of their appointment reservation. A prominent success badge pairs with an explicit reference code (*"APPT-2026-04127"*) and omnichannel dispatch notification (*"We've sent details to your email and SMS"*). An appointment summary card consolidates time, practitioner, venue, and required documents to bring (*Aadhaar, case papers, ID proof*). Actionable primary and secondary buttons (`Ux4gButton`) enable instant route navigation (**Get directions**) and digital device synchronization (**Add to calendar**).
