# Select Appointment Time

An interactive calendar scheduling and consultation slot selection pattern designed for government appointments, legal consultations, and public officer meetings.

The screen provides citizens with full temporal visibility over booking availability. An intuitive date & time picker trigger summarizes the active selection (`"18 Oct 2026 · 10:00 AM"`), while the underlying calendar slot grid (`Ux4gTimeslot`) maps daily statuses across standard government conventions: available booking days, fully booked dates (*No slots*), statutory gazetted holidays (*Public holiday*), and non-working weekends (*Weekly off*). Tapping an open calendar date seamlessly presents available morning and afternoon intervals in an accessible time-slot sheet, concluding with a primary action button (`Ux4gButton`) to advance into final confirmation.
