# Select Advocate

An advocate directory and legal consultation scheduling pattern designed for public legal aid, dispute resolution, and citizen advisory portals (such as the District Legal Services Authority and Tele-Law programs).

The screen assists citizens in finding the most suitable legal practitioner by displaying the administering authority and total advocates available (*"District Legal Services Authority, Pune · 12 available"*). Each advocate profile card presents crucial qualifications at a glance: practitioner name, domain specialization, years of practice experience, supported languages for multilingual consultations, and an immediate visual indicator of open appointment capacity (*"8 slots"*). Citizens can select an advocate via a full-width primary action button (`Ux4gButton`) to proceed directly to time slot selection.
