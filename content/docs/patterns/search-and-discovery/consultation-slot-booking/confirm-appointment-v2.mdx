# Confirm Appointment V2

A pre-confirmation verification pattern designed to eliminate booking errors, set clear consultation logistics, and provide citizens with final transparency prior to statutory appointment reservation.

The screen presents a structured appointment overview card summarizing the appointment date & time, assigned legal practitioner, physical room/office jurisdiction (*Block C, Room 12, District Legal Services Authority, Pune*), and consultation format (*In-person · 30 minutes*). An urgent, high-visibility warning banner alerts citizens to the temporary 5-minute reservation hold window, preventing unintentional slot abandonment. Clear hierarchical CTAs allow immediate confirmation via a primary button (`Ux4gButton`) or friction-free backtracking via a secondary **Choose different slot** action.
