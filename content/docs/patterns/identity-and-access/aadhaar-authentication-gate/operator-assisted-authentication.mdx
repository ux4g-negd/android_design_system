# Operator-assisted authentication

An assisted in-person authentication pattern designed for citizen service centers, Common Service Centers (CSC), and Seva Kendras. A certified Village Level Entrepreneur (VLE) operator assists citizens who face digital literacy barriers or biometric capture challenges. The screen displays verified operator credentials (*"Ramesh Kumar"*, VLE ID, MeitY certification), requires explicit citizen consent via an interactive checkbox (*"I consent to operator-assisted Aadhaar authentication. My identity documents have been verified by the VLE."*), and provides paired **Cancel** and **Proceed with Consent** actions along with a pinned Digital India brand footer.
