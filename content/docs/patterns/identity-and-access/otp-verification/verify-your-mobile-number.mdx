# Verify your mobile number

A focused mobile-number OTP verification pattern for citizen authentication. The screen features a branded government header, two-line heading typography (*"Verify your mobile\nnumber"*), masked mobile phone display (*"OTP sent to +91 98765 XXXXX"*), 6 auto-advancing `Ux4gOtpInput` digit boxes with integrated reverse countdown timer (*"Didn't receive OTP? Resend OTP in 00:58"*), primary **Verify OTP** button, **Change mobile number** link, and a pinned Digital India brand footer.
