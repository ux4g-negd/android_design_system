# Create your account

A citizen registration initiation pattern designed for government services. The screen features a branded government app header, a clear registration prompt (*"Enter your mobile number to get started"*), a structured Mobile Number input with a **+91** country prefix, a primary **Send OTP** call to action, an **"Already have an account? Sign in"** navigation shortcut, and a pinned Digital India brand footer.
