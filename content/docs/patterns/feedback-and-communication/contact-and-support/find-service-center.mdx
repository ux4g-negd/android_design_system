# Find Service Center

A location discovery pattern that assists citizens who require physical, in-person support in locating their nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or administrative facilitation desk.

The pattern pairs a geographic search bar (`Ux4gSearchField` with voice search and instant submit) with structured facility cards that display precise venue naming (*"CSC Sector 12 — Pune City"*), full civic address (*"45 Patel Nagar, Sector 12, Pune 411001"*), spatial proximity (*"1.2 km away"*), and verified operating hours (*"Open Mon-Sat 9 AM – 6 PM"*).

The pattern is available in two structural configurations:
1. **Default Style**: Clean, direct layout situated on a neutral white canvas featuring the official government portal header, location search bar, nearby centre card, navigation actions, and Digital India branding.
2. **Card Style**: An elevated card variant situated on a soft lavender canvas (`0xFFE2DCFF` / `Ux4gColors.primary100` in light mode, `primary800` in dark mode) with enclosed card container and footer attribution.
