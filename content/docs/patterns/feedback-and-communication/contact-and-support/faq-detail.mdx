# FAQ Detail

A contextual citizen assistance pattern that presents detailed troubleshooting guidance organized under specific public service categories (*Application Issues*, *Documents and Uploads*, *Payments and Fees*).

The pattern includes hierarchical breadcrumbs (`Home > Help > Application Issues`) that anchor citizens within the knowledge taxonomy, coupled with an interactive accordion list (`Ux4gAccordionGroup`). Citizens can expand individual questions to read step-by-step procedures, SLA expectations, and appeal mechanisms without being overwhelmed by dense text walls.

The pattern is available in two structural configurations:
1. **Default Style**: Clean, direct layout situated on a neutral white canvas featuring the official government portal header, breadcrumb navigation, expandable question accordion with dividers, and Digital India branding.
2. **Card Style**: An elevated card variant situated on a soft lavender canvas (`0xFFE2DCFF` / `Ux4gColors.primary100` in light mode, `primary800` in dark mode) with enclosed card container and footer attribution.
