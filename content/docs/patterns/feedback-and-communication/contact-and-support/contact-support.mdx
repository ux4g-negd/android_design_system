# Contact Support

A multi-channel assistance directory pattern that provides citizens with multiple accessible touchpoints for resolving escalations, clarifying procedural queries, and obtaining personalized support.

The pattern organizes support avenues into distinct channel cards—toll-free telephone support, real-time live chat with SLA wait indicators, asynchronous email inquiries, and physical Common Service Centre (CSC) walk-in locator buttons—alongside an integrated grievance escalation link (*"File a complaint about this portal"*).

The pattern is available in two structural configurations:
1. **Default Style**: Clean, direct layout situated on a neutral white canvas featuring the official government portal header, channel action cards with direct CTAs, and Digital India branding.
2. **Card Style**: An elevated card variant situated on a soft lavender canvas (`0xFFE2DCFF` / `Ux4gColors.primary100` in light mode, `primary800` in dark mode) with enclosed card container and footer attribution.
