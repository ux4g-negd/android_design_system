# Was This Helpful Alert

A lightweight in-situ feedback collection pattern that prompts citizens to rate the utility and clarity of knowledge base articles, scheme advisories, or transaction guidelines immediately after reading.

The pattern overlays a non-intrusive floating alert card at the bottom of the article view (*"Was this helpful?"*), presenting binary thumbs-up/thumbs-down response actions (`Ux4gButton` with outline styling). This provides immediate editorial feedback to government content teams without interrupting the citizen's browsing workflow.

The pattern is available in two structural configurations:
1. **Default Style**: Clean, direct layout situated on a neutral white canvas featuring the official government portal header, simulated help article content, bottom floating feedback card, and Digital India branding.
2. **Card Style**: An elevated card variant situated on a soft lavender canvas (`0xFFE2DCFF` / `Ux4gColors.primary100` in light mode, `primary800` in dark mode) with enclosed card container and footer attribution.
