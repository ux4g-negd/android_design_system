# Language Switcher

An essential citizen accessibility pattern that enables citizens across India's linguistic landscape to interact with digital public services in their preferred regional language.

The pattern presents a prominent interface language selector (`Ux4gSelectionDropdown`) featuring bilingual labels displaying both native scripts and English phonetics (*"हिंदी (Hindi)"*, *"मराठी (Marathi)"*, *"தமிழ் (Tamil)"*, *"తెలుగు (Telugu)"*, *"বাংলা (Bengali)"*, *"English (United States)"*). It guarantees immediate real-time locale switching without requiring a destructive full page reload or loss of active form session data.

The pattern is available in two structural configurations:
1. **Default Style**: Clean, direct layout situated on a neutral white canvas featuring the official government portal header, language selection dropdown, browser auto-detect disclaimer, and Digital India branding.
2. **Card Style**: An elevated card variant situated on a soft lavender canvas (`0xFFF2EFFF` / `Ux4gColors.primary100` in light mode, `primary800` in dark mode) with enclosed card container and footer attribution.
