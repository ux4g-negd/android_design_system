# Translation Unavailable

A transparent fallback communication pattern designed to manage citizen expectations when a specific public service or procedural page has not yet been translated into their selected regional language.

Rather than silently falling back to English without explanation or blocking service access with an error code, the **Translation Unavailable** pattern clearly informs citizens of the current localization status (*"This page is not yet available in Tamil"*), provides an estimated delivery timeline (*"We are translating Income Certificate pages — the Tamil version is coming in May 2026"*), and surfaces actionable continuation pathways (`Switch to English` and `Translate with Browser` via `Ux4gButton`).

The pattern is available in two structural configurations:
1. **Default Style**: Clean, direct layout situated on a neutral white canvas featuring the national portal header, localized status messaging, prominent amber progress card, dual primary/outline continuation buttons, and Digital India branding.
2. **Card Style**: An elevated card presentation situated on a soft lavender canvas (`0xFFF2EFFF` / `Ux4gColors.primary100` in light mode, `primary800` in dark mode) enclosing the translation alert within a rounded container.
