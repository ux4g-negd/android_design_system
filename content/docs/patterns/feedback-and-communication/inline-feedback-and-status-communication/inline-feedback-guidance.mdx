# Inline Feedback Guidance

A context-sensitive guidance and inline status communication pattern that dynamically adapts form requirements and instructions based on a citizen's previous selections (such as requiring a caste certificate upload when selecting an affirmative reservation category).

Rather than overwhelming citizens with a static, exhaustive list of prerequisites upfront or surprising them with rejection errors at submission, this pattern introduces conditional input fields, document upload dropzones, and alert banners in real time. This ensures citizens understand exactly why supplementary documents are required and when they will be processed.

The pattern is available in two structural configurations:
1. **Default Style**: Clean, direct form layout anchored by the official government portal header, back navigation, category selector, conditional file upload area, alert banner, and a full-width bottom progression button.
2. **Card Style**: An elevated card container situated on a soft tinted purple background (`Ux4gColors.primary100` / `0xFFF2EFFF` in light mode, `primary800` in dark mode) with pinned bottom progression controls.
