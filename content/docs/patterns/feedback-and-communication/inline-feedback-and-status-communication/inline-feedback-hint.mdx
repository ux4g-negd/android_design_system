# Inline Feedback Hint

An inline guidance and status communication pattern that provides citizens with proactive instructional hints during high-stakes credential intake (such as entering a 12-digit Aadhaar number for identity verification and personal detail pre-filling).

Rather than remaining passive until an error occurs, the pattern offers an encouraging, informative hint below the field (*"Enter 12 digits — we will prefill your name and address"*). When citizens begin entering their UIDAI credential, the input automatically formats chunks (`XXXX XXXX XXXX`), validates the Verhoeff checksum algorithm in real-time, and seamlessly transitions to inline validation confirmation upon completion.

The pattern is available in two structural configurations:
1. **Default Style**: Clean, direct form layout anchored by the official government portal header, back navigation, guidance header, informative helper hint, and a full-width bottom verification button.
2. **Card Style**: An elevated card container situated on a soft tinted purple background (`Ux4gColors.primary100` / `0xFFF2EFFF` in light mode, `primary800` in dark mode) with pinned bottom progression controls.
