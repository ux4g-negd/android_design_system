# Consent Capture (Consent Not Given)

An essential compliance and data protection guardrail pattern presented when a citizen attempts to proceed or navigates the consent screen without selecting any permission scopes or credentials.

Under Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) principles, consent cannot be assumed or defaulted. When zero permissions are granted:
- A prominent amber **warning banner** informs the citizen that without consent, no personal information can be shared and the digital journey cannot proceed.
- The primary **Allow** action is **disabled** to prevent accidental or incomplete authorization.
- The secondary **Deny** action remains fully active, providing a clear and dignified exit path.
